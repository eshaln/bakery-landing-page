OUR STORY

Every recipe starts with someone we love.

Eshal's Bakistry began the way most good things do - in a small kitchen,
with too much flour on the counter and no intention of turning it
into a business.

From one cake to a full case.

It started with a birthday cake for a friend, then a cousin's wedding order, then a line of neighbors asking for "just one more batch" of cupcakes. Eshal never set out to open a bakery - she just kept saying yes to the next order.

Today, every cake, cupcake, and donut we sell is still made the same way it was on day one: from scratch, in small batches, with nothing rushed. We haven't scaled up the shortcuts - just the ovens.
-Eshal, founder

2021

Baked our first cake order from a home kitchen for a friend's birthday.

2022

Word of mouth turned into a weekly cupcake stand at the local market.

2024

Opened our first storefront - still family-run, still scratch-made.

2026

Now baking daily for a neighborhood that's become part of the family.
